Freedom Fighters

Major General K. M. Shafiullah

মেজর জেনারেল কে এম সফিউল্লাহ

Commander of Sector 3 & 'S' Force; Bir Uttom

The Joydebpur Uprising & Sector 3 Operations

"We knew that once we revolted in Joydebpur, there was no going back. We were fighting not just for our military lives, but for our motherland’s right to exist as a sovereign democracy."
Read Full Testimony & Transcript
Biographical Overview

Second-in-command of 2nd East Bengal Regiment in Joydebpur. Led his troops into rebellion on March 29, 1971, and organized guerrilla warfare across Habiganj, Kishoreganj, and Brahmanbaria.

Transcribed Excerpt

"Guerrilla operations in Sector 3 were grueling. The monsoon flooded the haors. Our boys operated from small country boats, ambushing Pakistani supply convoys under pitch darkness."

Foreign Correspondents

Sir Mark Tully, KBE

স্যার মার্ক টালি

Chief of Bureau, BBC South Asia (1971)

Reporting the War for the BBC World Service

"Every evening in East Bengal, entire villages gathered around a single battery-powered radio, holding their breath in the dark to hear the BBC chimes. The hunger for truth was overwhelming."
Read Full Testimony & Transcript
Biographical Overview

Veteran British broadcaster who reported continuously on the refugee camps, international diplomacy, and the military liberation of Dhaka on December 16, 1971.

Transcribed Excerpt

"We entered Dhaka with General Aurora's helicopter convoy on December 16th. Thousands of people running alongside shouting 'Joi Bangla' with tears of relief is something I will never forget."

Freedom Fighters

Taramon Bibi, Bir Protik

তারামন বিবি, বীর প্রতীক

Freedom Fighter, Sector 11 (Kurigram Front)

From River Chars to the Battlefield: A Woman's Courage

"They told me the war was too dangerous for a teenage girl. I said: the bullets do not ask if you are a man or a woman; they only ask whose soil you are defending."
Read Full Testimony & Transcript
Biographical Overview

Joined the Mukti Bahini at age 14 in Kurigram under Havildar Tarikullah. Scouted enemy positions in disguise and participated in heavy armed combat along the Brahmaputra River.

Transcribed Excerpt

"I took my rifle and joined the trench line. The gunboats shelled us for hours, but our fighters stood firm until the enemy withdrew."

Diplomats & Witnesses

Archer K. Blood

আর্চার কে ব্লাড

US Consul General in Dacca during 1971

The Moral Imperative: Witnessing Operation Searchlight

"To sit in our consulate windows and hear the artillery shells slamming into dormitories only a few hundred yards away, knowing our government chose silence, made duty a profound moral crisis."
Read Full Testimony & Transcript
Biographical Overview

American diplomat stationed in Dhaka who authored the famous "Blood Telegram" dissenting against the official US foreign policy of complicity during the 1971 crackdown.

Transcribed Excerpt

"We knew that sending Telegram 1136 would end our diplomatic careers under Kissinger, but we could not remain silent accomplices to slaughter."

Cultural Activists

Syed Hasan Imam

সৈয়দ হাসান ইমাম

Broadcaster, Shadhin Bangla Betar Kendra

The Radio of Freedom: Inspiring a Resisting Nation

"Songs like 'Joy Bangla Banglar Joy' and M. R. Akhtar Mukul's satirical 'Chorompotro' gave our freedom fighters more energy in the trenches than a thousand rations."
Read Full Testimony & Transcript
Biographical Overview

Leading actor and cultural activist who co-organized the clandestine broadcasts of Shadhin Bangla Betar Kendra from Kalurghat and Calcutta, sustaining national morale.

Transcribed Excerpt

"We hung blankets on studio walls to muffle street noise. Artists recorded patriotic choruses with sweat pouring down their faces."

Political Leadership

Tajuddin Ahmad

তাজউদ্দীন আহমদ

First Prime Minister of Bangladesh (Mujibnagar)

Organizing the Wartime Government & War Effort

"We are not fighting for power; we are fighting for the survival of seventy-five million human beings whose fundamental right to liberty has been met with tanks and bayonets."
Read Full Testimony & Transcript
Biographical Overview

Architect of wartime governance who organized the Mujibnagar Government, coordinated international diplomacy with Indira Gandhi, and oversaw the Mukti Bahini war operations.

Transcribed Excerpt

"Our victory is dedicated to the millions of ordinary farmers, students, and workers who picked up rifles to protect the dignity of our people."
