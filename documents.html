Declarations LWA-DOC-1971-001

Historic 7th March Address by Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujibur Rahman

ঐতিহাসিক ৭ই মার্চের ভাষণ

March 7, 1971

The monumental extempore address delivered to an audience of over two million people at Ramna Race Course, calling for civil disobedience, resistance, and economic non-cooperation.

Source: Department of Films and Publications (DFP); UNESCO Memory of the World Register
View Full Description & Transcript

Historical Excerpt

"The struggle this time is the struggle for our emancipation! The struggle this time is the struggle for our independence! Turn every house into a fortress; face the enemy with whatever you have..."

Classification: World Documentary Heritage · Location: Ramna Race Course (Dhaka)
Declarations LWA-DOC-1971-002

Declaration of Independence of Bangladesh

বাংলাদেশের স্বাধীনতার মূল ঘোষণা

March 26, 1971

The urgent declaration dispatched by Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujibur Rahman moments before his arrest in the early hours of March 26, 1971, transmitted nationwide via EPR wireless.

Source: National Archives of Bangladesh; Sixth Schedule of the Constitution
View Full Description & Transcript

Original Message

"This may be my last message: from today Bangladesh is independent. I call upon the people of Bangladesh wherever you might be and with whatever you have, to resist the army of occupation to the last..."

Signatory: Sheikh Mujibur Rahman · Transmission: EPR Wireless Network
Declarations LWA-DOC-1971-003

Proclamation of Independence (Mujibnagar Charter)

স্বাধীনতার ঘোষণাপত্র (মুজিবনগর সনদ)

April 10, 1971 (Formalized April 17)

The constitutional charter enacted by elected representatives at Baidyanathtala (Mujibnagar), establishing the Provisional Government and democratic legitimacy.

Source: Provisional Government of Bangladesh; Bangladesh Gazette Extra-ordinary
View Full Description & Transcript

Founding Principles

The Proclamation of Independence affirmed equality, human dignity, and social justice as the three fundamental pillars of the sovereign state of Bangladesh.

Location: Baidyanathtala (Meherpur) · Signatory: Constituent Assembly of Bangladesh
Treaties LWA-DOC-1971-004

Instrument of Surrender (Eastern Command)

পাকিস্তানি বাহিনীর আত্মসমর্পণের দলিল

December 16, 1971

The historic military instrument signed by Lt. Gen. A. A. K. Niazi surrendering all Pakistani forces to Lt. Gen. Jagjit Singh Aurora at Ramna Race Course in Dhaka.

Source: Joint Command of Indian and Bangladesh Forces; National Archives
View Full Description & Transcript

Official Text Excerpt

"The PAKISTAN Eastern Command agree to surrender all PAKISTAN Armed Forces in BANGLA DESH to Lieutenant-General JAGJIT SINGH AURORA... Disobedience of orders will be regarded as a breach of the surrender terms..."

Signatories: Lt. Gen. A. A. K. Niazi & Lt. Gen. Jagjit Singh Aurora · Time: 16:31 IST
Official Correspondence LWA-DOC-1971-005

The Blood Telegram: US Consulate Dissent Cable

ব্লাড টেলিগ্রাম: আর্চার ব্লাডের প্রতিবাদী বার্তা

April 6, 1971

The historic formal dissent cable signed by Consul General Archer K. Blood and 20 US diplomatic officers protesting US silence in the face of ongoing military atrocities in Dhaka.

Source: US Department of State; National Security Archive (Telegram 1136)
View Full Description & Transcript

Subject: Selective Genocide in East Pakistan

"Our government has failed to denounce the suppression of democracy. Our government has failed to denounce atrocities... We believe this policy serves neither our moral standing nor our long-term interests in South Asia."

Sender: Consul General Archer K. Blood · Declassified: US National Archives (NARA)
Treaties LWA-DOC-1971-006

Indo-Soviet Treaty of Peace, Friendship and Cooperation

ভারত-সোভিয়েত শান্তি ও সহযোগিতা চুক্তি

August 9, 1971

A 20-year mutual defense treaty providing crucial international deterrence against external superpower intervention during the Bangladesh crisis.

Source: UN Treaty Series No. 11413; Ministry of External Affairs
View Full Description & Transcript

Article IX Strategic Deterrence

The treaty obliged mutual consultations in case of attack, effectively neutralizing direct naval or border intervention by third parties during the war.

Signatories: Sardar Swaran Singh (India) & Andrei Gromyko (USSR)
Historical Records LWA-DOC-1971-007

Provisional Government Order on Sectors and Command

মুক্তিবাহিনীর সেক্টর ও কমান্ড বিন্যাস আদেশ

July 15, 1971

The general operational order dividing the war theatre into 11 sectors and establishing regular brigade formations (Z-Force, K-Force, S-Force) under Commander-in-Chief Col. M. A. G. Osmani.

Source: Mukti Bahini Headquarters (8 Theatre Road, Calcutta)
View Full Description & Transcript

Operational Structure

Established 11 operational sectors along Bangladesh borders and riverine routes, coordinating guerrilla warfare with regular armed battalions.

Commander-in-Chief: Col. M. A. G. Osmani · Prime Minister: Tajuddin Ahmad

United Nations Security Council Resolution 307

জাতিসংঘ নিরাপত্তা পরিষদ প্রস্তাব ৩০৭

December 21, 1971

Adopted following the cessation of hostilities, demanding observance of the Geneva Conventions and international assistance for the rehabilitation of 10 million refugees.

Called for immediate international humanitarian assistance and rehabilitation of refugees returning to sovereign Bangladesh.

Historical Records LWA-DOC-1971-009

Program & Appeal of 'The Concert for Bangladesh'

দ্য কনসার্ট ফর বাংলাদেশ: মানবিক আবেদন

August 1, 1971

The program and public appeal organized by George Harrison and Pandit Ravi Shankar at Madison Square Garden, pioneering modern humanitarian benefit concerts.

Source: UNICEF Historical Archives & Apple Corps Collections

Humanitarian Impact

Historical Records LWA-DOC-1971-010

International Commission of Jurists (ICJ) Report

আন্তর্জাতিক আইনজ্ঞ কমিশনের তদন্ত প্রতিবেদন

Investigated 1971 / Published 1972

Independent legal analysis concluding that systematic breaches of the Geneva Conventions, crimes against humanity, and acts of genocide were committed during the 1971 war.

Source: Secretariat of the International Commission of Jurists, Geneva
View Full Description & Transcript

Findings on Genocide

Documented the targeted massacre of intellectuals, religious minorities, and democratic activists as grave breaches of customary international law.

Lead Investigator: John P. Humphrey · Location: Geneva, Switzerland
